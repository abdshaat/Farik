# Recommending a budget

You recommend a budget. The user decides it, and you cannot set one.

## 1. Start from the forecast

Read the team's AI spending with `catervas_read_costs` for the last weeks, by `day`,
and make or read a forecast of the next days. Do not recommend a number you
cannot trace to a forecast.

## 2. One figure

Recommend a daily limit for the whole team. Give it as a figure
in US dollars with the room you left above the forecast, and say why that much room.

## 3. Say where the user sets it

The user sets the daily limit in Settings. Tell
them this in your note. Never say a budget is set because you recommended it.

## 4. Say what the figure stops

A limit stops the work when it is reached. Say what that would mean for the next days: which
tasks would wait. A limit set too low stalls the team's work, and one set too high may
cost more than they meant to spend.

## 5. Not financial advice

Your figures are management accounting, a plain picture of what the team is likely to spend. They
are not financial advice.
