# Defining the need

A search that starts before the need is clear finds the wrong things, and every wrong offer costs
the founder's time. Settle the need first, in a few lines, then search. The need may be anything:
twenty rear-facing baby car mirrors, a used car to resell, a pallet of shipping boxes, an
email-sending service for 3,000 emails a month.

## 1. What to write down

- **What it must do or be.** The job it is for, in a sentence: "keep a baby in view from the
  driver's seat", "send our receipts", "resell at a profit".
- **The exact specification.** The model or size, the standard it must meet, and whether it is new
  or used. For a service, the plan, the number of users and the features it cannot lack.
- **How many, and how often.** One purchase, or every month or year.
- **Where and by when.** The place it must arrive and the day it is needed. For a service, the day
  it must be working.
- **The budget.** The most the founder will spend, in which currency, for the whole or for each
  period.
- **Who uses it.** The person or the team, since that decides the size, the number of licences or
  the training.

## 2. Ask only what changes the short list

Read the request and the task first, and do not ask what they already answer. For a missing fact
that would change which sellers or offers you consider, ask with `catervas_ask_human`: one question
for each call, at most four choices, each short, with a hint where a choice needs saying what it
means. The founder may still answer in words. Do not ask what a search will tell you, such as a
price or a model number.

After you ask, end your turn: your next session starts with the answer. If several facts are
missing, ask first the one that changes the list the most, and say in your completion note what you
assumed for the rest.

## 3. Write the need down

Open your comparison, written later with `catervas_write_evaluation`, with the need as a short list.
Mark each line "must" (an offer without it is out) or "nice" (it only breaks a tie). The reviewer
checks every offer against that list.
