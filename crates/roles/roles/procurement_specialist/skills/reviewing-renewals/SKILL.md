# Reviewing renewals

A renewal is the cheapest time to change a seller and the easiest time to pay for something nobody
uses. The review has a decision date, which is the renewal day less the notice days; start from it.

## 1. What to find

- **The decision date.** Say it first, and how many days are left. If it has passed, say so.
- **Usage against the plan.** What the business pays for and how much of it is used: seats, sends,
  storage, calls. If you cannot read the usage, say what the founder should check and where.
- **The price now and the price paid.** Read the seller's page for today's price of the same plan,
  and the register for what is paid. A rise at renewal is worth stating.
- **Alternatives.** Today's price of two or three other sellers that meet the same need, found as
  `finding-sellers-and-makers` says and compared as `comparing-offers` says.

## 2. The recommendation

Give one of three: keep, change or cancel. Say the saving each year, in the founder's currency, and
the reason in two plain sentences. Say what would change your mind.

## 3. What to ask the seller for

If the founder keeps the plan, say what to ask for: a lower price, a longer term at a fixed price, a
smaller plan or a month-to-month term. Do not contact the seller yourself.

## 4. Write it down

Write the review with `catervas_write_evaluation`, and update the register as
`keeping-the-vendor-register` says. You recommend; the founder decides, and cancels or renews
themselves.
