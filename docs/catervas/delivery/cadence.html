Sprint cadence

How this team works in sprints, as of the retro of S1. Only what the board, the team rules and the retro facts show is written here; anything not shown is left out.

What S1 showed

  • S1 planned one task, CTV-11 (catervafy the repository). The human cancelled it because the work was already documented. S1 delivered nothing and spent $0.07.
  • CTV-9 (ready) and CTV-10 (refining, waiting on the owner's answer) were not in S1 and remain open.

Ceremonies

  • The team plans a sprint from the candidates on the board, reviews it against the board, and holds a retro. The Scrum Master runs each and posts the result to the team channel.
  • Sprint length and calendar days are not set in the team rules; none is recorded here.

Budget

  • The team rules set no per-task budget cap (max_task_budget_usd is none). No sprint budget is recorded.

Work in flight

  • The Scrum Master assigns a ready task to an agent of the assignee role that has room under the team's WIP limit. If nobody has room, the task stays ready and the note says why.
  • A lane's tasks all go to that lane's builder.

Waiting and the owner

  • A task that is not chosen at planning stays on the board in its status (ready or refining) until a later planning.
  • The owner is asked one clear question when only they can answer, such as which read limit CTV-10 should lift. Contracts that need human approval wait in escalation until the owner decides.
  • CTV-9 proposes removing sprints. If it is accepted, this document changes at the next retro.
