# Marketing plan: Catervas, first plan

Dates: 2026-10-12 to 2027-01-04 (12 weeks). Currency: USD. Budget: 0 in total, 0 for Google Ads.

## Where this comes from

Only from `docs/catervas/product/` (spec and roadmap, read 2026-10-11). Nothing was researched on the web and no brand kit, persona or research notes exist yet; this plan says so rather than guessing.

## The situation

Catervas runs a small team of AI agents (Product Manager, Developer, Marketing Specialist and others) against one git repository for one person. A rule-checker written in code, the "governor", approves every action. The roadmap says there is no release yet: the web launch (`v0.1.0`, open source) is phase 13, after the role kits, the first real-user tests and a published benchmark. So this plan does not sell a product. It explains an idea and builds a small first audience, and it never says Catervas can be downloaded or used today.

## Who it is for

From the product plan, in its order: the non-technical founder or creator first; then the solo builder; then the small-team lead (2-5 people). Small-business owners come later (phase 14) and enterprise is deliberately deferred, so neither appears in this plan.

## Goals

1. Publish eight posts on the plan's calendar (read 2027-01-04).
2. Learn which of four messages draws the most clicks: what it is, why teams of agents fail, plain-language control for founders, honest status (read 2027-01-04).
3. Meet at least some real people in the named audiences, counted by replies or messages (read 2027-01-04). No target is set because we have no baseline.

## Messages (each from the product plan)

- Multi-agent failures are mostly organisational, not model capability.
- Contracts before work; the reviewer is never the author; the governor checks every permission, budget and path.
- A non-technical person can set up and run a team in the web app, with no terminal.
- Local first, on your own key; sessions are bounded and hitting a limit asks you.

We do not cite the roadmap's cost estimate (about $25 for a first sprint day) in posts: the spec calls it an estimate, not a cap.

## Channels

LinkedIn for founders and team leads; X and Bluesky for builders. Chosen from the audience in the product plan alone, not from channel research, so the owner should confirm which accounts exist and are connected. Any channel not connected drops out and its posts move to a connected one.

## Budget

Zero. There is no launch to advertise, no evidence yet of what a click costs, and no ad account named. No Google Ads campaigns are proposed. A paid test is better proposed once a first release date is set and research on search terms exists.

## Assumptions and gaps

- The product plan does not name the product's website, a sign-up page or a call to action; posts have no link until the owner supplies one.
- No launch date exists, so no countdown or launch campaign is planned.
- Brand kit and persona are missing. A later task should write them, and the voice of the posts should be reviewed once they exist.
- No competitor or market facts are used; a research task should come before any paid spend.
