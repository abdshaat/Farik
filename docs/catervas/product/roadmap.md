# Catervas roadmap

Taken from the project's plan, its decisions and its recent history. Catervas has no public release yet.

## Now

- **Finishing the role kits.** The phase that gives every role its own skills and services ends with its product-safety and eBay sources; as of 9 October, four parts built on 2 October still wait for their final review before the phase is merged.
- **The founder's live runs of the kits.** The founder's own runs of most kits in the web app are still open, and move to the Catervas Cloud phase.
- **Changing the project from the browser.** Just merged: the person can point the team at another project from the web app, with the team carried over fresh and the project's name always on screen.
- **The new name.** Farik became Catervas everywhere but the historical record, and tasks are now numbered CTV.

## Next

- **Catervas Cloud.** A free service run by Catervas that signs people in to GitHub in one click and to Google Ads, with the website, the privacy policy and a Catervas account; its hosting is planned privately, apart from this project.
- **Ask or auto.** The owner chooses to approve every outward action or let the team act on its own within its limits, with purchases, spending limits and the owner's decisions always kept.
- **The kit check.** Each role but the DevOps Engineer does one real task with its own tools in the web app, recorded and signed off.
- **The first real-user tests.** A recorded team sprint in the browser, then the thirty-minute test with five new users, at least three of them non-technical.

## Later

- **Any AI account and agent program.** Connect providers other than Claude and other agent programs, open-source ones included, under the same rules, with every kit checked again on each.
- **The ecosystem.** Memory history and a decisions view, a viewer for the full record, browser notifications, the hooks for paid features, and an optional DevOps Engineer that deploys, watches production and restores a failed deploy.
- **The benchmark.** A published, blind comparison of a Catervas team with one fully equipped agent, which must pass before launch.
- **The web launch.** The first public open-source release, version 0.1.0, as the web app alone, on macOS, Windows and Linux.
- **Business workspaces.** Catervas for any business, software or not, with teams that need no Developer.
- **Desktop.** A desktop app with the pixel-art office, started only after real users have tested the web release.
- **Phone apps.** Native iOS and Android apps after the desktop app; how a phone reaches a running Catervas is still open.
- **Premium.** Paid cloud hosting of customers' workspaces and agents, and Slack.
