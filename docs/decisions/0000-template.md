# NNNN. Title in sentence case

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN | deprecated

## Context

What situation forces a decision. Facts, constraints, and the options that were realistically on the table. Two to six sentences per option.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, in the active voice.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what this commits us to. Include the thing that cuts against the decision; a record with no downside was not thought through.
