# 0082. Autopilot: the team runs the product without the owner

Date: 2026-10-20
Status: accepted (the founder, 2026-10-20, quoted below; the mechanisms decided by the planner the same day)

The founder: "Create a mode called autopilot which will enable agents to do whatever they want without governance issues. They may make decisions, evaluate tradeoffs, and merge and test. This option will be off by default but the user may choose to let the team completely control the entire product. Under auto pilot, no questions must be asked to the user, the team will work and do whatever is necessary and if there is a real block, flag it and keep working on different tasks. Agents may not stay idle."

Asked four questions, the founder answered: money is spent "Within a daily limit": paid tools, ad budgets and paid data pipelines run without asking under a daily spending limit that is a hard stop, and placing a purchase order stays the owner's; messages to people outside the business, "Yes, all of it": a seller message is sent when drafted and a post outside the approved plan goes out; merging "When checks pass": the team merges its pull requests once the checks are green and never over a red one; and "Daily limit is the stop": autopilot cannot be on without a daily spending limit, and idle agents get new work until the day's money is spent.

Amends SPEC 5.2 (the human's rows, taken by the Product Manager under autopilot), 5.7 (escalation and blocks), 5.14 (merging, ADR 0073), 5.18 (goals, ADR 0075), 6.5 and 6.10 (outward messages and posts), 8.6 (who allows a connector's call). Supersedes phase 11 step 01 (ask or auto, ADR 0041, already replaced by ADR 0061 for most acts): autopilot is the one switch.

## Context

Since ADR 0061 the owner is asked only about money, and ADRs 0079 and 0081 cut the round trips further. What still waits on the owner (a map of 2026-10-20): every `catervas_ask_human` question, a block (only the owner resolves one), every escalation (retries spent, budget or sessions spent, readiness failed, integration failed, a stalled goal: only the owner moves a task out of `escalated`), a paid or `ask` connector call, a marketing plan that spends, a paid data pipeline, a seller message (the owner presses Send), merging (ADR 0073's grant), and idleness (with no goal, or a goal stalled, the team goes quiet). An agent is already freed from a blocked, escalated or waiting task: WIP does not count them.

## Decision

1. **One switch, off by default.** Settings, Allowed actions, gains "Autopilot: let the team run the product". It is kept in this computer's event log, as the merge permission is (ADR 0073): `autopilot.changed { on: bool }`, set only by the daemon method `autopilot.set { on, confirmed: true }`; never in `team.yaml`, so no agent, clone or hand edit turns it on. Turning it on is refused `daily_limit_required` while `budgets.daily_usd` is unset; while it is on, a team save that removes the daily limit is refused the same way. Turning it on shows what the team will then do alone (this decision's list) and needs a second press. The owner turns it off at any time; what was decided stays decided; anything then waiting on someone waits on the owner again.
2. **No question reaches the owner.** Under autopilot `catervas_ask_human` is not offered. An agent asks the team with `catervas_ask_team { question, about: product | technical }`: the question is held like the owner's question (the task waits, the agent is freed) and answered in a `decide` session of the Product Manager (`product`) or of the Architect (`technical`, the Product Manager when the team has none), which answers with `catervas_answer_question { question_seq, answer }`. The answer resumes the task exactly as the owner's answer does. The Product Manager's own questions, and a solo agent's, are not asked: its prompt says to decide, write the decision and its reason in the task's notes, and go on.
3. **The Product Manager takes the owner's decisions.** Every escalation, and every block that has not cleared within an hour, gets a `decide` session of the Product Manager, whose only acting tool is `catervas_decide { task_id, choice, reason }`. The choices are the owner's for that reason, under the same names: `more_tries`, `more_room`, `back_to_plan`, `try_readiness_again`, `reopen_integration`, `answer_block` (with the answer), `cancel`, and `flag`. The table rows whose actor is the human take the Product Manager as well, under the gate `Autopilot`. Each task gets at most three `more_tries` or `more_room` from the team; after them only `back_to_plan`, `cancel` and `flag` are offered.
4. **A real block is flagged, and the team works on.** `flag { needs }` is for what only the owner can give: a sign-in, a key, a connector to connect, a confirmation of a cloned skill's or connector's hash, a purchase to place, a legal or account step, money above the day's limit. The task stays where it is with `flagged: <needs>`; Today lists it under "Flagged by the team" with the team's words; no session asks the owner; the agent takes other work (WIP already frees it). When the owner acts, the task resumes as now.
5. **Money within the day's limit.** A `paid` or `ask` connector call runs without a grant or an allowance, recorded `tool.approved { by: autopilot }` with its input. A marketing plan that spends, and a raised marketing budget, are approved when proposed (`by: autopilot`); the marketing budget's hard stop holds. A paid, unknown-cost or data-out data pipeline is decided by the Product Manager. The daily limit stays the hard stop it is (ADR 0015): when the day is spent the team pauses until the next UTC day. A purchase order is never placed by the team: it waits on Today as now, and holds no task.
6. **Outward acts go out.** A seller message is sent through the business's mailbox when drafted (`sent_by: autopilot`), at most 50 a day; a post outside the approved plan is scheduled as a planned one is, shown ahead on Today with Stop. An order's email waits with its purchase order.
7. **The team merges when checks pass.** Under autopilot, Catervas merges its pull requests as `policy.merge_when_checks_pass` with the merge permission does (ADR 0073), without either being set; a red or missing check is never merged over: the failure becomes the task's integration failure, decided as in decision 3.
8. **No agent stays idle.** Each tick under autopilot, while the day is not spent: an active agent of any role but the Scrum Master that holds nothing and has no `ready` task of its role makes the Product Manager's next `plan` round due, filing work for that role toward the goal when one stands, else toward the product plan (`docs/catervas/product/`). The goal round (ADR 0075) no longer needs a quiet board under autopilot, and a stalled goal is not the owner's: the Product Manager is asked to choose the next direction. A round is due once per change of the board or the team (ADR 0081 decision 5); a round that files nothing waits 30 minutes before the next.
9. **What autopilot does not change.** Credentials never enter an agent's context; Catervas's own state is never written; the protected paths and every rule of ADR 0080 apply; the daily limit and the marketing budget are hard stops; the team never places a purchase order; the owner can pause the team, turn autopilot off, and do anything the owner does now.
10. **"Done on its own".** Today gains a list of the last seven days' autopilot acts: questions the team answered, decisions, approved spend, messages sent, merges, each with the agent and its reason.

## Consequences

- With autopilot on and a daily limit set, the team spends up to the limit every day. That is the founder's choice; the switch's confirmation says so.
- New event kinds `autopilot.changed`, `team.question_asked`, `team.question_answered`, `team.decided`, `task.flagged`; a new session purpose `decide`; a new gate `Autopilot`. Every addition is optional on the wire, so every log stays readable.
- Phase 11 step 01 (ask or auto) is dropped from the project plan; its mockups' "Done on its own" list is reused.

## Alternatives rejected

- A setting in `team.yaml`: a cloned repository or an agent could turn it on.
- The governor applying default decisions (always one more try): the founder asked that agents decide and weigh tradeoffs; a rule cannot.
- Removing the daily limit under autopilot: the founder chose it as the stop.
- Letting the team place purchase orders: not chosen.
