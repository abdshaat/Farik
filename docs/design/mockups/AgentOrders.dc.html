1. Ivo’s page. “Orders” sits just before “Sites it may read” (grey bars stand for the parts of the page that do not change). Ivo suggests orders and follows them up; you place and pay. An approved order waits for you to place it, with the day it closes by itself if you don’t. A placed order shows what Ivo learned from its last follow-up, with Ivo’s words as text, or your correction in your words; one past its expected day says “Overdue”. A placed order never closes by itself, because you have paid: it ends when you mark it received, or say it won’t come. Below, the last orders that ended, and how.
