Plan E-3. Every plan with parts waits for your approval before anyone builds it.

Mira, your Product Manager, wrote this for you

Customers will be able to buy a gift card, send it to a friend by email, and use it when they order. Cards never run out, as you said.

I left out printed cards and gift cards for the café counter. We can plan those later if you want them. The whole plan should cost about $14 to build.

The plan in 4 parts

The green line under each part is how Farik will know it is done.

  1. 1
    Buy a gift card A customer picks $25, $50 or $100, pays, and gets a receipt. Done when a test purchase of each amount goes through and the receipt shows the right amount.
  2. 2
    Send it to a friend The card arrives by email with a code and a short message. Done when a test email arrives with a working code, and a wrong address is caught before paying.
  3. 3
    Use it when ordering At checkout the customer types the code and the balance comes off the total. Done when a card pays for part or all of an order, and a used-up card is refused with a clear message.
  4. 4
    Tell customers Kai writes the announcement post and a line for the menu page. Done when you have read and accepted the post.

Not in this plan

  • Printed gift cards
  • Gift cards at the café counter
  • Changing how prices are worked out
