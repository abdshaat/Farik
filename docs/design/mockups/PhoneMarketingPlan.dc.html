# MP-3 Autumn at Corner Bakery

## What I found
- Last year you sold 74 Thanksgiving pies, 61 of them to people who already shop here.
- Three bakeries within two miles take pie pre-orders. Two advertise on Google from early November; none posts more than once a week.
- People near you search "thanksgiving pie near me" about 1,900 times in November, and "bakery near me" about 2,400 times a month (Google Ads keyword ideas, 5 October 2026).
- A click on those searches costs $0.90 to $1.60 here.
- Your Instagram has 1,240 followers. Morning photos of the counter do best: about three times the likes of other posts.

## Goals
1. 120 pie pre-orders by 22 November.
2. New faces on Saturday mornings.

## Channels and budget
Instagram, two posts a week, and X, one a week: no cost.
Google Ads, $450 in all:
- "Pie pre-orders", 26 October to 22 November, $300: searches for Thanksgiving pies within 5 miles.
- "Bakery near me", 12 October to 22 November, $150: searches for a bakery within 2 miles, mornings only.

## Calendar
Weeks 1 and 2: the autumn menu and the people who bake it.
Weeks 3 to 6: pie pre-orders, with a reminder each week and the pick-up days.

## Measures
Pre-orders from the order page and the counter book, each week.
Cost per pre-order from Google Ads' conversions.
Followers and saves from Buffer's post results.
