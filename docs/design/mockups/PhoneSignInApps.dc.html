Google Ads shows your ads to people searching on Google and charges you for the clicks.

Why Kai wants it

So the Marketing Specialist can find the words your customers search for and run the search ads in a marketing plan you approved, within its budget.

What to do

Sign in with the Google account that manages your ads and allow Farik to manage them. Farik makes and changes search ads only inside a marketing plan you approved, never deletes anything, and never touches billing or who can use your account. The ads cost money at Google, up to the budget in your plan.

Google lets Farik sign in only with a Google app of your own. Make it once, and every agent on this computer can sign in with it.

  1. 1On Google Cloud, signed in to the Google account that manages your ads, create a project. Any name will do.Open Google Cloud’s new project page
  2. 2In that project, turn on the Google Ads API: choose “Enable”.Open the Google Ads API’s page
  3. 3Open Google Auth Platform and choose “Get started”. Give your app a name and your email. For its audience, choose “External”, or “Internal” if your business uses Google Workspace.Open Google Auth Platform
