Get Farik See how it works

For people with a product in mind and no time to build it.

You know what your customers need. Farik’s team turns that into working software, one small, checked piece at a time, on your own project and your own computer.

You talk to it like you would talk to a team: ask for something, answer a few questions, approve the plan, and accept the result when it is done.

Meet the team

  • PM Product Manager Turns what you ask for into a clear plan, and asks you when something is unclear.
  • SM Scrum Master Hands out the work, runs the team’s short meetings, and keeps everyone unblocked.
  • A
    ARCH Architect Keeps the product well built, and reviews the code before you see it.
  • DEV Developer Builds and tests what the plan asks for. The only one who changes code.
  • MKT Marketing Specialist Researches your market and writes the posts, pages and plan to reach it.

Rename them, change their pictures, or add a second developer. A team is two to seven agents.

How it works

  1. Step 1 You ask Say what you want in your own words: “let customers buy gift cards.” No forms, no code.
  2. Step 2 You approve the plan Your Product Manager asks what it needs to know, then shows you a plan in plain language. Nothing is built until you say yes.
  3. Step 3 The team builds it The work is handed out, built, tested, and reviewed by a second agent. You can watch on the board or not at all.
  4. Step 4 You accept the result You read a short summary and see Farik’s own checks. The code changes are one click away if you want them.

A team you can leave alone

Every agent works inside rules that Farik enforces, not rules it hopes they follow.

Farik checks the work itself

Every task has a list of things that must be true when it is done. Farik runs those checks on its own and does not take the team’s word for it.

You approve what is risky

Big requests and risky changes wait for your yes, both before the work starts and before it lands in your product.

Nothing leaves your computer by surprise

Agents cannot push code, send email or post anywhere unless you allow it. Secrets like passwords stay out of their reach.

You set the spending

Put a daily limit on what the team may spend on AI, or none at all. Farik shows every dollar, by agent and by task.

Open source, and free to run yourself

Farik is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence. The whole team, the rules that keep it safe, and the record of everything it did are free, forever, on your computer with your own AI key.

Nothing that makes the agents safer is ever a paid extra. Hosted plans that need no key of your own come later.
