## Goal

The Procurement Specialist suggests a purchase order and then tracks it; it never places, pays for, confirms or cancels one. It drafts an order (the seller, each line with its quantity and unit price, the currency, delivery and terms, the seller's page on an approved site, and the comparison behind it), which Farik writes as `orders/PO-<n>.xlsx` in its folder. The founder approves it ("I'll place it myself") or rejects it on Today or at the command line, places and pays for it themselves, marks it placed on the agent's page (with what they paid, if they know), and marks it received when it comes. Between the two the agent follows up through what it may reach and records what it learns (being prepared, shipped, delayed, a problem); the founder can correct any status. An order not decided within 30 days of its drafting, or approved and not marked placed within 30 days of its approval, closes by itself; a placed order never does, and shows as overdue. "Mark placed" and "Mark received" each offer to ask the agent for the follow-up or the register's update, in a request the founder reads and may edit. Once a day, with no model, Farik reads the register's renewal dates and puts each renewal whose decision date is two weeks off or nearer on Today. Out of scope: Farik or the agent paying, placing, confirming or cancelling an order (ADR 0039: never); sending an order or a message to a seller (step 10f); follow-ups that run without a task; Farik writing the register (the agent does); reminders by email or notification (phase 9 step 03).

## Decisions

- **A purchase order is a record and a document, not a gate.** `farik_draft_purchase_order` writes the order's workbook and records `purchase_order.drafted`; it does not end the session, hold the task, or ask before running, unlike an `external_effect` call (ADR 0031), because nothing outside Farik happens. The task goes on; the order waits for the owner alone, as a post outside the plan does (5.7). Rejected: tagging it `external_effect` and reusing `tool_approval`, which would make the approval a permission for Farik to act when the founder, not Farik, places the order.
- **Suggest, then track: who records which step.** An order is drafted (the agent), approved or rejected (the owner), placed (the owner), then received or closed (the owner), or expired (Farik); from placing to its end it also has a follow-up status, recorded by its own agent from what it learns or by the owner correcting it. Every step but the drafting and a status counts only from an event whose envelope names no agent and no session, as `marketing_plans` (`store/marketing.rs:98`) and `decision_on` (`store/waiting.rs:194`) count only the owner's. No Farik tool records a step, and none records what was paid. Rejected: the agent marking an order placed or received from what a seller's page says (a page is untrusted, and "only the founder marks an order placed or received").
- **The follow-up statuses**: `preparing` ("Being prepared": the seller is making or packing it), `shipped` ("Shipped"), `delayed` ("Delayed") and `problem` ("A problem": out of stock, a payment refused, cancelled by the seller). Each carries `note`, 0 to 300 characters on one line (1 to 300 for `delayed` and `problem`, the reason), and `expected_on`, the day the seller expects it to arrive, ISO, not before the day it is recorded, optional except for `delayed`. Rejected: `confirmed` (it reads as the agent confirming the order, which it may never do); `delivered` (only the owner says it came); `cancelled` (the owner closes the order; a seller's cancellation is a `problem` the owner reads).
- **Its input**, `{ seller, seller_contact, lines, currency, period, delivery, terms, url, evaluation, why }`, checked in this order, each refusal named, nothing written or recorded on any: only a Procurement Specialist in the implement session of a task it is the assignee of (`in_its_own_implement_session`, `tools/sheets.rs:647`; `purchase_order_refused`); `seller` 1 to 100 characters on one line with no control character (`purchase_order_seller_invalid`), `seller_contact` 0 to 200 likewise (an address, a phone number or a name, shown as text); `lines` 1 to 50 (`purchase_order_lines_invalid`), each `{ item, quantity, unit_price, unit }`, `item` 1 to 200 characters on one line, `quantity` an integer from 1 to 1,000,000, `unit_price` what `farik_core::marketing::parse_amount` reads (no sign or separator, at most eight whole digits and two decimals), `unit` 0 to 20 (`purchase_order_line_invalid` with the line's index and field); the total, exact in hundredths (`Amount`), at most 10,000,000.00 (`purchase_order_too_large`); `currency` `^[A-Z]{3}$` (`purchase_order_currency_invalid`); `period` `once`, `month` or `year` (a subscription's lines are per period); `delivery` and `terms` 0 to 600 each; `url` empty, or an address `site_of` accepts (`https`, a named host, no user name, password or port; at most 2,000 characters; `purchase_order_url_invalid` with `site_of`'s words) whose site is approved for the team (answer 1: in `approved_sites(log, farik hosts)`, Farik's sites not turned off and the owner's), else `purchase_order_site_not_approved: <host> is not a site the owner allowed; ask for it with farik_request_sites first, then end your turn`; `evaluation` a relative path `evaluations/<name>.md` that `private_path` passes and that exists (`evaluation_missing`); `why` 20 to 600 characters; then no open order for the same `seller` (without regard to case) on the same task (`purchase_order_open`), an order being open until it is rejected, received, closed or expired; then at most 20 drafted orders waiting for the owner in the project (`too_many_purchase_orders`; step 10b2's 20 requests are the precedent). An empty `url` is a seller with no page, met by phone or in person.
- **The number and the workbook** (Blocking 1). One lock, `ORDERS` (`crates/runtime/src/procurement.rs`), is held from the first read to the record by the draft, by each of the owner's commands, by a status and by the expiry. The order's number `<n>` is one more than the highest among the log's `purchase_order.drafted` and the files `orders/PO-<n>.xlsx`, as `propose_plan` numbers `MP-<n>` under `NUMBERING` (`tools/marketing.rs:127`). Farik writes the workbook as a new file with `write_new_workbook`, a sibling of step 09b's `write_workbook` (`tools/sheets.rs:550`, whose `store_file` replaces) that never replaces (`purchase_order_file_exists` if one is there, as `write_new`'s `marketing_plan_file_exists`), then records `purchase_order.drafted { order: <n>, … }`, and removes the file if the record fails. `<n>` is the order everywhere: `PO-<n>`, every command's `order`, the waiting row's, `farik order approve <n>`. The workbook has one sheet, `Order`: a header block (order number, date, buyer, seller and contact, currency, period, delivery, terms), then a line table (item, quantity, unit, unit price, line total) and the total. Quantities, prices and totals are number cells Farik computes from `Amount`s, never formulas, and text is text, so the seller and the founder read the same numbers in any program. The buyer is `team.yaml`'s `name` (no business name exists until phase 12). `SheetInput`, `CellInput` and `DateCell` gain `pub(crate)` constructors, since Farik, not a tool's input, builds these sheets.
- **The agent never writes `orders/`.** `farik_write_sheet` refuses a path whose first part is `orders`, compared without regard to case (`Orders/PO-1.xlsx` on macOS), with `orders_are_farik_s`; `farik_read_sheet` reads the orders.
- **Events**, eleven kinds, `<entity>.<past_tense_verb>`, in `event.schema.json`, spec 8.5 and every exhaustive match (`EventBody`, `kind`, `body_def_name`, `EVERY_KIND`, 76 kinds today and 87 after, the names of `protocol/src/lib.rs`, the fixtures): `purchase_order.drafted { order, seller, seller_contact, lines: [{ item, quantity, unit, unit_price, line_total }], currency, period, total, delivery, terms, url, evaluation, why }`, amounts as two-decimal strings, the task, agent and session on the envelope; `purchase_order.approved`, `.rejected` and `.closed`, sharing `purchaseOrderDecisionBody { order, note }`, `note` required and empty when the owner said nothing (one body for several kinds, as `toolApprovalDecidedBody`), which leaves `{ order }` to `purchase_order.expired { order }` alone under the schema's `oneOf`; `purchase_order.placed { order, placed_on, paid?, currency? }`; `purchase_order.updated { order, status, note, expected_on? }`, its `status` one of the four above; `purchase_order.received { order, received_on, paid?, currency?, renews_on? }`; `renewal.flagged { vendor, renews_on, decide_by }`; `renewal.dismissed { renewal }`, `renewal` the seq of its `renewal.flagged`; `renewal.checked { due, unreadable }`, once per day's run. Each order event after the drafting carries the order's task on its envelope; the owner's and Farik's name no agent and no session; an `updated` the agent records names it and its session.
- **No migration** (Blocking 2). - **Only the owner decides, places, receives, closes and corrects**, with five commands on `POST /command` behind the daemon's token and in the browser's RPC behind the session cookie, as `tool_approve` (ADR 0031), each checking and writing under `ORDERS`, as `decide_tool_call` (`orchestrator/human.rs:478`) does under its lock: `purchase_order_decide { order, decision: approve | reject, note? }`; `purchase_order_place { order, placed_on?, paid?, currency? }`; `purchase_order_receive { order, received_on?, paid?, currency?, renews_on? }`; `purchase_order_close { order, note? }`; `purchase_order_update { order, status, note?, expected_on? }`, the owner's correction, under the statuses' rules (`purchase_order_status_invalid`). `paid` follows `unit_price`'s rule up to 10,000,000.00 (`purchase_order_paid_invalid`); `currency` is `^[A-Z]{3}$` (`purchase_order_currency_invalid`) and, absent beside a `paid`, the order's; dates are ISO, `placed_on` and `received_on` today (UTC) when absent; `note` at most 600 (`purchase_order_note_too_long`); the latest `paid` given, at placing or receiving, is what was paid. - **`farik_update_purchase_order { order, status, note, expected_on? }`**, the agent's follow-up: `read` tier, offered to the Procurement Specialist in the implement session of a task it is the assignee of, refused `purchase_order_update_refused` in any other session or role; `unknown_purchase_order`; `purchase_order_not_yours` for an order another agent drafted; `purchase_order_not_placed` for one not placed, received or closed; `purchase_order_status_invalid` for a status outside the four (`placed`, `received` and `paid` among them) or a `note` or `expected_on` its rule refuses. Statuses are on the agent's page and in `farik order list`, not on Today: they ask nothing of the owner. Rejected: a session the tick starts for each placed order each day (a model run with no task and no budget line).
- **Expiry** (answer 2, amended by "Suggest, then track"): every tick not scoped to a task, in `Orchestrator::tick_within` (`orchestrator.rs:464`) beside `end_marketing_plans`, before the pause is read (it starts no session, so a paused team has it too), and returning no `TickReport`, Farik records `purchase_order.expired { order }` under `ORDERS` for each order drafted 30 days ago or more and not decided, and each approved 30 days ago or more and not placed. Rejected: expiring only a cancelled task's drafted orders (it reads the board for one case and leaves a live task's order on Today for good); keeping a drafted order until rejected (one whose task ended would wait with nobody to act on it). No "Didn't buy it" button: an order not placed closes by itself.
- **A placed order never closes by itself.** It is `overdue` once its expected day has passed, the latest status's `expected_on` or, with none, 30 days after `placed_on`; the owner ends it with "Mark received" or "It won't come" (`purchase_order_close`: the seller cancelled or refunded it, or it was lost). The founder has paid: an order that closed by itself would hide money spent on goods that never came, which is what the follow-ups are for. Rejected: expiring it as an approved order expires.
