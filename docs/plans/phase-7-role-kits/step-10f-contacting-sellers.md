## Goal

The founder's answer of 2026-10-05 (O1): the Procurement Specialist "compiles list of sellers, look for price, contact manufacturer or sellers. Get prices. And set up a purchase order but the final decision is the founder". After this step the agent can draft a message to a seller or a maker, asking for a quote, a price list or an answer, and Farik sends it from a procurement mailbox the founder connects (a mailbox of its own or an alias of the founder's), when the founder presses "Send" after reading it, and may edit it first. Farik reads, every 15 minutes and with no model, only the messages addressed to that mailbox's address that answer a message it sent or come from an address it wrote to, and hands them to the agent as untrusted content; the founder reads them on Today. An order the agent suggested can be approved and emailed to its seller in one press, which records it placed; the founder pays the seller. Out of scope: `auto` (step 10h, which sends any drafted message but an order's without the press, to any address); Microsoft mailboxes (with Farik Cloud, phase 11); phone calls, contact forms and chat widgets (the agent tells the founder where only those exist); the founder's main mailbox beyond the alias rule; paying anyone.

## Decisions

- **A procurement mailbox of the user's own**, as the receipts mailbox of spec 6.6 is: a mailbox of its own or an alias of the founder's main account (answer 3), read over IMAP and sent from over SMTP with an app password. Rejected: the main mailbox through Google's or Microsoft's mail API, for 6.6's reasons (a restricted scope with a paid yearly assessment, a grant over the whole mailbox); a connector of the kit, since sending is Farik's act on the founder's press, not a tool the agent calls.
- **The alias rule** (answer 3). An alias's sign-in reaches the whole mailbox, so a check opens the folder the settings name (default `INBOX`) with `EXAMINE`, read-only, and fetches every new message's headers alone (`UID`, `RFC822.SIZE`, `BODY.PEEK[HEADER.FIELDS (FROM TO CC DELIVERED-TO MESSAGE-ID IN-REPLY-TO REFERENCES SUBJECT DATE)]`). It fetches a body (`BODY.PEEK[]`) only when `is_for_us` holds: the procurement address is in its To, Cc or Delivered-To, compared without regard to case; and its In-Reply-To or References holds the `Message-ID` of a sent message, or its From address is one a sent message went to; and its size is at most 25 MB. Every other message is skipped on its headers, its body never fetched. Nothing is ever marked read, moved or deleted. The setup copy says the login can reach the whole mailbox and what Farik reads. Spec 6.6's "alias" stays.
- **Providers** (answer 4), a fixed table chosen on the page or found from the address's domain: Gmail and Google Workspace (`gmail.com`, `googlemail.com`: `imap.gmail.com:993` and `smtp.gmail.com:465`, both TLS; app passwords need 2-Step Verification and are unavailable with security keys only or Advanced Protection, and may be missing on work accounts, Google support 185833); iCloud Mail (`icloud.com`, `me.com`, `mac.com`: `imap.mail.me.com:993` TLS, `smtp.mail.me.com:587` STARTTLS, Apple 102525; an app-specific password, Apple 102654); Fastmail (`fastmail.com`, `fastmail.fm`: `imap.fastmail.com:993`, `smtp.fastmail.com:465`, both TLS; its Basic plan has no IMAP); "Another provider", the servers typed. The choice "Outlook.com or Microsoft 365", and an address at `outlook.com`, `hotmail.com`, `live.com` or `msn.com`, are refused `mailbox_provider_unsupported` before any connection: Outlook.com stopped app passwords for mail programs on 2024-09-16, Exchange Online's IMAP password sign-in is off since 2022-10-01, and its SMTP password sign-in goes off by default at the end of December 2026 (MC786329); Microsoft's sign-in comes with Farik Cloud (ADR 0044). A Microsoft 365 address on the business's own domain cannot be told from its name; its login fails, and the login refusal's words name Microsoft. Rejected: Proton through its Bridge (127.0.0.1 with a self-signed certificate, which the TLS rule refuses, and paid plans only); Zoho in the table (its servers differ by plan and region, so it is "Another provider").
- **Encryption.** Each server is `tls` (encrypted from the start) or `starttls`, from the table, else `tls` on 993 and 465 and `starttls` on any other port; never plain. Rejected: the port alone deciding, which would demand STARTTLS of the fixture's implicit TLS on 3993 and 3465. `starttls` refuses to log in unless the server upgraded first (`mailbox_needs_tls`). The certificate is checked with `rustls-platform-verifier` (locked at 0.7.1) and nothing turns that off; `Trust::Root` gives a test its root as a parameter, the product always `Trust::Platform`. Each server step has a 30-second timeout (`mailbox_unreachable`).
- **The crates**, pinned exactly, licences in Task 1's report: `lettre = "=0.11.23"` (MIT) with `default-features = false` and `builder`, `hostname`, `smtp-transport`, `tokio1`, `tokio1-rustls`, `rustls-platform-verifier`, `aws-lc-rs` (its default TLS is native-tls); `async-imap = "=0.12.0"` (MIT or Apache-2.0) with `default-features = false` and `runtime-tokio` (its default runtime is async-std); `mail-parser = "=0.11.9"`; `tokio-rustls = "=0.26.6"` and `rustls-platform-verifier = "=0.7.1"` directly (each only locked through other crates today, on rustls 0.23.45 with aws-lc-rs). This is the IMAP and SMTP plumbing phase 13 step 02's receipts intake reuses.
- **Connecting.** `procurement_mailbox.connect { address, name, provider, imap, smtp, username, password, folder, signature, disclose_ai }`, a method of `daemon/team.rs`'s `METHODS` in `rpc.schema.json` beside `connector.connect`, behind the session cookie, whose refusal carries no data, as `account.connect`'s: `provider` `gmail`, `icloud`, `fastmail`, `other` or `microsoft`; `imap` and `smtp` each `{ host, port, security }`; `address` one address `lettre`'s `Address` parses; `name` 1 to 100 characters on one line (the From name and the disclosure's); `folder` 1 to 100 on one line; `signature` 0 to 600; `disclose_ai` on by default (`mailbox_settings_invalid` naming the field). It logs in to both servers (IMAP: `EXAMINE` the folder, reading `UIDVALIDITY` and `UIDNEXT`), sends nothing, then keeps the password as a `Secret` (`runtime/claude.rs:88`, whose `Debug` prints `[redacted]`) in the keychain, service `farik`, account `mailbox:<local_project_id>:procurement`, or without a keychain in `connectors.json` under that key, through `MailboxSecrets`, a store beside `ConnectorSecrets` (whose key is per agent), and records `mailbox.connected { purpose: procurement, address }`. Connecting again replaces all of it. `procurement_mailbox.disconnect {}` deletes the password and `mail/mailbox.json`, keeps `mail/out/` and `mail/in/`, and records `mailbox.disconnected { purpose: procurement }`. `procurement_mailbox.get`, a query, answers `{ connected, address?, name?, provider?, folder?, checked_at?, error?, restarted_at?, sent_today, cap }`. Refusals in Farik's words: `mailbox_login_failed`, `mailbox_needs_tls`, `mailbox_certificate`, `mailbox_unreachable`, `mailbox_provider_unsupported`.
- **Storage** (Blocking 3). In `.farik/local/procurement/mail/`: `mailbox.json`, the settings with no password; `ledger.json`, `{ uidvalidity, last_uid, checked_at, error?, restarted_at? }`, which starts at `UIDNEXT - 1` when the mailbox is connected, so older mail is never read, and starts there again when `uidvalidity` changes; `out/<n>.txt`, a draft's body; `out/<n>.sent.txt`, the subject line, a blank line and exactly the text Farik sent; `in/<yyyy-mm>/<r>/text.txt`, a reply's text, and `<i>.pdf`, `.png` or `.jpg` named by their bytes, never by the seller's file name, which is only ever text. One lock, `MAIL` (`procurement.rs`, beside `ORDERS`), an async mutex since a send holds it across the server, is held by drafting, sending, discarding, the check and connecting; a number is one more than the highest among the log's and the files', taken under it, and a file is written new, never replaced. `copy_baseline` and `changes_since_baseline` leave `mail/` out, as they leave `.history` out (Blocking 2): Farik writes it, not the task. `farik_write_sheet` refuses a path whose first part is `mail`, without regard to case (`mail_is_farik_s`), as 10c's `orders_are_farik_s`.
