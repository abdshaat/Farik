## Goal

At a sprint ceremony (planning, review or retro), an agent whose role owns a folder keeps its small recurring documents there with `catervas_write_folder_doc`. A document written for agents becomes a folder change: one commit, authored for the agent, on a branch of its own made from the integration branch (`docs/folder-<n>`), recorded `folder_doc.written`, and integrated under the team's `integration` policy exactly as an accepted task's branch is (5.14): merged and pushed, put in a pull request, or left for `catervas integrate folder-<n>`. The product's `spec.md` and `roadmap.md`, which the owner approves, are proposed at the sprint review instead (`folder_doc.proposed`, with their `.agent.md` version and a summary); the owner approves the review's proposals, which makes one folder change of both files of each (`folder_doc.approved`), or sends them back with notes (`folder_doc.returned`) that the agent's next sprint review reads as the owner's own words. The owner decides with `folder_doc_decide`, on `POST /command` or the browser's `command`. Out of scope: Today's card, its query, the agent's "Waiting on you" line and the line in "What moved" (step 03b); a docs task that may change the spec or the roadmap accepted by the owner (03b); the Files page, the owner's edits, staleness and the re-derive task (04); the Today row of a folder change waiting under `manual` (03b); telling the agents to use the tool at the review, and the Architect's and the Product Manager's review sessions (07); the marketing plans, which keep their own flow (ADR 0042) and got their twin in step 01.

## Decisions

- **Split.** Project plan row 03 becomes two steps: 03, this one (the tool, the events, folder changes and their integration, the owner's decision and the review's line), and 03b (Today's card and its query, the agent's waiting line and the moved line, and the owner accepting a docs task that may change the spec or the roadmap). Rejected: one plan of about 450 lines.
- **The tool**: `catervas_write_folder_doc { path, text, agent_text?, summary? }`, tier `read`, placed after `catervas_ask_human` in `TOOLS` (`crates/runtime/src/tools.rs:183-427`), where step 01 removed `catervas_write_product_doc`, so `TOOLS` is 48 long again and its read slice `[..41]` (`tools.rs:809`). Its description, exactly: "At a sprint planning, review or retro: write a document of your own folder under docs/catervas/. A document for agents is committed on a branch of its own and added to the project as the team's integration policy says. The product's spec.md and roadmap.md are proposed to the owner at the sprint review, with agent_text (their .agent.md version) and summary (what changed and why, for the owner), and wait for the owner's approval." Tier `read` because ceremony sessions change nothing (01b) and the tool writes nothing in the session's tree: Catervas commits, by this step's rules. It has no `paths_of` arm (`tools.rs:558`), so `Call::permit` (`tools.rs:653`) checks only the tier. Rejected: `write_workspace`, which 01b keeps from every session that is not a task's implement session.
- **Where it is offered.** `offered_tools` (`crates/runtime/src/orchestrator/session.rs:853`) offers it, as `WRITE_FOLDER_DOC_TOOL`, only to a `ceremony` session whose `thread` is `planning`, `review` or `retro` and whose agent's role has a folder (`role_folder`). `PLANNING_TOOLS` (`crates/runtime/src/orchestrator/rules.rs:584`), `CEREMONY_TOOLS` (`:679`, the review's and the standup's) and `RETRO_TOOLS` (`:457`) list it; the thread keeps it from the standup. Ceremonies are run by the Scrum Master, or by the Product Manager on a team with none (`assigner`, `rules.rs:1505`); step 07 gives the Product Manager and the Architect review sessions of their own.
- **The path**, pure in core (`check_folder_doc_path`): repository-relative, normalised by `normalise` (`crates/core/src/governor/paths.rs:87`; one it refuses is outside), compared with letter case; a `.md` file under the caller's folder, at least one segment below it (`folder_doc_path`); not an `.agent.md` (`folder_doc_path`: the twin is written from `agent_text`); not a path the team protects (`check_protected_paths`, `paths.rs:69`, of the path and of its twin when it has one; `folder_doc_path`). A human document outside `OWNER_ACCEPTED`, which is a marketing plan, is refused `folder_doc_marketing_plan`: it is proposed with `catervas_propose_marketing_plan` in a task (ADR 0042). A path through a link is refused when Catervas commits (`folder_doc_link`).
- **`OWNER_ACCEPTED`** (`catervas_core::folders`): `docs/catervas/product/spec.md` and `docs/catervas/product/roadmap.md`, the human documents the owner approves through this tool (and, in 03b, through a task's acceptance).
- **The fields.** `text` is the document (for a pair, the version for people), not blank and at most 262,144 bytes (`folder_doc_empty`, `folder_doc_too_large`), 256 KiB being the Files page's cap. For a path in `OWNER_ACCEPTED`, `agent_text` (the `.agent.md`, the same bounds) and `summary` (1 to 300 characters once trimmed) are required, and for any other path each is refused (`folder_doc_agent_text`, `folder_doc_summary`). `summary` (the controller's ruling of 2026-10-10) is not in the design's `{ path, text, agent_text? }`: the approved card shows the agent's own words above the changes ("Pie pre-orders are done, so they move to Done …"), which nothing else carries; 03b shows a card's summaries in proposal order. Rejected: no summary, which leaves the card as drawn unbuildable.
- **A human document is proposed at the sprint review alone** (the controller's ruling of 2026-10-10): a proposal from a session whose thread is not `review` is refused `folder_doc_at_review`. It names the sprint the review is about, `ended_sprint` (`crates/runtime/src/ceremonies.rs:268`), which a review session always has; none is `folder_doc_at_review` too. Rejected: any ceremony, whose card could not say which review it came from.
- **A folder change** (the controller's ruling of 2026-10-10) is one commit Catervas makes for folder documents: an agent's write here, an approval here, the owner's edit in step 04. `land` (`crates/runtime/src/tools/folders.rs`), its caller holding the integration lock, numbers it `n`, the first number above the log's highest `change` (the fold's `changes`) for which git has neither `docs/folder-<n>` nor `origin/docs/folder-<n>` (`Git::tree`, `crates/store/src/git.rs:233`, failing for both), so a fresh clone never reuses a branch; makes `docs/folder-<n>` from the integration branch's head (`integration_branch`, `git.rs:733`) with the one commit, by `Git::commit_files`. It answers `None` when every file already says its text on the integration branch, making nothing. **B1, the controller's decision of 2026-10-10:** `folder_doc_waiting` ("<path> has a change waiting to be added to the project (folder-<n>)") holds a path only while its change is neither integrated nor escalated. A later change to an escalated change's path is made from the integration branch as usual; integrating the older change afterwards is the human's call (`catervas integrate folder-<n>`), and a conflict then escalates like any other. Under `manual`, a folder change waiting to be integrated is listed on Today exactly as a task awaiting integration is (`waiting.rs:465`'s `integration` rows, extended from tasks to folder changes in step 03b: same row component and button, its title the document's plain name and the sprint review it came from), so no new screen is drawn and no channel line is posted for it. The branch name cannot clash with a task's (`docs/CTV-<n>`, 5.14).
- **`Git::commit_files(branch, from, files, message, author)`** (new, `git.rs`) works in the root's checkout as `merge` (`git.rs:489`) does, the commit's own answer first: in order, `Linked(path)` when a file, or a directory between the root and it, is a symbolic link (`symlink_metadata`); `Busy(paths)` when `git status --porcelain=v1 -z -- <paths>` names any of them, staged, changed or untracked, so Catervas never carries off or writes over the owner's change made outside it (`folder_doc_busy` says to commit or put it aside first); `Unchanged` when every file's text equals `file_at(from, path)` (`git.rs:243`; a file git does not have there differs); otherwise `git checkout -b <branch> <from>`, each file written (its directories made), `git --literal-pathspecs add -- <paths>`, `git commit --only -m <message> [--author <author>] -- <paths>` made as `as_someone` (`git.rs:580`), so nothing else the owner staged is committed and the committer is the person git knows there, then the previous branch checked out again; `Committed { sha }`. `author: None` commits as git's own identity, which step 04's owner's edit takes. Refused before anything when HEAD is detached. On any failure after `checkout -b`: each written path is restored (`git checkout <from> -- <path>`, or removed when new) and unstaged, the previous branch is checked out, and `git branch -D <branch>` runs; the answer is git's error. Rejected: plumbing that commits without a checkout, which no other Catervas commit does.
- **Integration, as an accepted task's** (the controller's ruling of 2026-10-10). A folder change is integrated by the routine that integrates an accepted task's branch, `integrate_locked` (`crates/runtime/src/orchestrator/integrate.rs:146`, the policy's choice at `:182-186`), reached on a tick through rule 2 (`awaiting`, `integrate.rs:44`, called at `rules.rs:185-194`) and on the human's word through `integrate` (`integrate.rs:74`; `Orchestrator::integrate`, `crates/runtime/src/orchestrator.rs:564`): `auto_merge` merges it with a merge commit and pushes (`merge`, `:492`); `pull_request` pushes the branch and opens a pull request (`open`, `:414`), then reads its state on each tick (`through_the_forge`, `:307`); `manual` leaves it for the human. The routine takes a task id; the smallest change is a subject in its place, `enum Subject { Task(TaskId), FolderChange(u64) }`, threaded through `attempt` (`:120`), `integrate_locked`, `integrations_of` (`:203`), `through_the_forge`, `closed_for_the_human` (`:369`), `open`, `merge`, `record_integrated` (`:542`), `escalate` (`:563`), `since_accepted` (`:590`), `escalated_since_accepted` (`:614`) and `last_integrated_sha` (`:626`). A task's integration is unchanged. For a folder change: its branch is `docs/folder-<n>`; its merge message `Merge docs/folder-<n>: <paths>` and its pull request's title `Folder change <n>: <paths>` (paths within `docs/catervas/`, joined by `, `); the pull request's body its paths, one per line, then "Approved by the owner on Today." for an approval; its own events stand for the task's (`folder_change.integrated` for `task.integrated`, `folder_change.opened` for `pull_request.opened`, `folder_change.escalated` for an integration escalation, which also posts Catervas's line in the channel with its detail, since an escalation is about a task and Today lists those); "since accepted" reads as since the change was recorded; and the scan refresh (`refresh_the_scan`, `:217`) is not run, a folder change touching no language, toolchain, tests or criterion. A second tick rule beside rule 2, in a tick scoped to no task and after the accepted tasks, attempts each change not integrated, as `awaiting` attempts each accepted task (passed over under `manual`, and after an escalation until the human's word), and reports `TickReport::FolderChange { change, what }` (`what` as a task's: "integrated at <sha>", "opened the pull request <url>", "escalated its integration to the human: <detail>"). Rejected: a push of its own after the commit, which bypassed the team's policy; and a task per change, which the lifecycle's review and criteria do not fit.
- **The human's word**: `folder_change_integrate { change }` (`command.schema.json`, `Command::FolderChangeIntegrate`), handled by `Orchestrator::integrate_folder_change`, whatever the escalations, as `catervas integrate <task>` is; `catervas integrate` (`crates/cli/src/lib.rs:499`, mapped at `:1569`) takes `folder-<n>` beside a task id. Refused `no_such_folder_change`; a change already integrated answers its sha, as a task does.
- **What the tool answers.** A written document answers `{ path, change, branch, next }`, `next` by the team's policy: `auto_merge` "Catervas merges <branch> into <into> on its next tick."; `pull_request` "Catervas opens a pull request for <branch> on its next tick; the document is in <into> once it merges."; `manual` "<branch> waits for the owner to add it." Nothing says "at once". Then `folder_doc.written { path, written_by, change, sha }` is recorded. Text the integration branch's file already holds is refused `folder_doc_unchanged`, nothing made.
- **`land(deps, team, files, message, author)`** answers `Ok(Some(Landed { change, branch, sha, next }))`, `next` the sentence below for the team's policy, `Ok(None)` for unchanged, or `LandRefusal::{Busy, Link, Waiting, Git}`, mapped to `folder_doc_busy`, `folder_doc_link`, `folder_doc_waiting` and the call's failure with git's words.
- **Author and message**, pure in core: the author is `<display name> (Catervas) <catervas@localhost>`, the name with `<`, `>` and control characters removed, or the agent's id when nothing is left (`folder_doc_author`); the commit's message is `docs(<the folder's last segment>): <each path within the folder, joined by ", "> by <display name>`, with `, approved by the owner` after an approval (`folder_doc_message`).
- **The integration lock** (`LOCK` and `lock`, `integrate.rs:32`, `:646`) moves to `catervas_runtime::transitions::integration_lock`, beside `integration_branch`'s re-export (`crates/runtime/src/transitions.rs:47`); integrate's `lock` calls it and maps its error as now. The tool and the decision hold it from their checks to their event, so a write, an approval and an integration never interleave on the root's checkout (5.14). `ponytail:` the tool and the command take the file lock on the async thread, as `git::commit` already runs git there; `spawn_blocking` as `attempt` does (`integrate.rs:120`) if a ceremony stalls on it.
- **A proposal** is `folder_doc.proposed { path, text, agent_text, summary, sprint_id, proposed_by }`, its seq the proposal's number, as a `tool_approval.requested`'s seq is the approval's. Nothing is written. A text and a twin both equal to the integration branch's files are refused `folder_doc_unchanged`. The tool answers `{ proposal, next: "The owner approves it or sends it back on Today; you are told at your next sprint review." }`. A newer proposal of the same path supersedes an older one still pending, with no event of its own: the fold reads it from the order of the two. Rejected: `folder_doc.withdrawn`, a kind for what the log already says. The proposal holds no task, so nothing waits on it: the session ends as any ceremony's.
- **The owner decides**: `folder_doc_decide { proposals, decision: approve | return, note? }` (`command.schema.json`, `Command::FolderDocDecide`), on `POST /command` behind the daemon's token or the browser's `command` behind its cookie, as `data_pipeline_decide` (`crates/runtime/src/orchestrator/human.rs:1134`). `proposals` holds 1 to 20 distinct numbers, a card's; `note` at most 600 characters, the reason for `return` (blank: `folder_doc_reason_needed`), optional for `approve`. Under the integration lock, all or nothing, the log read again first: `unknown_folder_doc` (a number that is no proposal), `folder_doc_decided`, `folder_doc_superseded`, `folder_doc_mixed` (two agents' proposals; a card is one agent's). Approve: one `land` of every proposal's two files (the path with `text`, its twin with `agent_text`), authored for the proposing agent (its id when it has left the team), `folder_doc_busy`, `folder_doc_link` and `folder_doc_waiting` refusing it whole with nothing recorded; then `folder_doc.approved { proposals, change?, sha?, note? }`, `change` and `sha` absent when the files already said it all and nothing was made. Its `said`, by policy, is `land`'s `next` for the change. Return: `folder_doc.returned { proposals, reason }`, nothing made. Neither event has an agent, a session or a task on its envelope, and the fold ignores a decision that has, as `is_the_owners` reads it (`crates/runtime/src/orchestrator/messages.rs:357`). Only the owner decides, under `ask` and `auto` alike (ADR 0041); a proposal whose agent was paused or retired since may still be decided. Refusals use `order_refusal` (`human.rs:841`).
- **The owner's words reach the agent at its next sprint review.** The review session of `review_and_retro` (`rules.rs:367`, facts at `:424-429`) gets, after `sprint_review_message`'s facts, one line per owner's decision of its runner's proposals recorded since that runner's last `session.started` with thread `review`, oldest first: "The owner approved your changes to <paths, joined by ", ">." followed by " The owner adds: <note>" when there is one, or "The owner sent back your changes to <paths>: <reason>". The words are not wrapped: they are the human's own (ADR 0011), as a returned marketing plan's are (`messages.rs:276-293`). After them, one line per proposal of its runner's still pending, oldest first: "Your change to <path> (proposal <n>) still waits for the owner; proposing it again replaces it." Rejected: every next session of the agent, most of which cannot propose again.
- **Events**, in `docs/schemas/event.schema.json`, after `seller_reply.dismissed` in `EVERY_KIND` (`crates/protocol/src/event.rs:434`, 101 → 108) and `KINDS` (`crates/protocol/src/lib.rs:20`): `folder_doc.written { path, written_by, change, sha }`, `folder_doc.proposed { path, text, agent_text, summary, sprint_id, proposed_by }`, `folder_doc.approved { proposals, change?, sha?, note? }`, `folder_doc.returned { proposals, reason }`, `folder_change.opened { change, url, number }`, `folder_change.integrated { change, sha, into, integrated_by }` (`governor` or `human`, as `task.integrated`'s), `folder_change.escalated { change, detail }`. `path` matches `^docs/catervas/[^/]+/.+\.md$`, at most 512 characters; `text` and `agent_text` 1 to 262,144 characters; `summary` 1 to 300; `sprint_id` `^S[1-9][0-9]{0,5}$`; `proposals` 1 to 20 distinct integers from 1; `change` and `number` integers from 1; `note` at most 600; `reason` and `detail` 1 to 600 and 1 to 4,000; `sha`, `into` and `url` at least 1 character. `attribution` (`event.rs:326`) gives `written_by` and `proposed_by`; the decisions and the three `folder_change.` kinds name no one in the body, as `marketing_plan.approved` and `task.integrated` do not. `is_about_one_contract` (`event.rs:249`) is false for all seven: a folder change is about no task. The design's `{ path, agent }` is `written_by`, the field `attribution` reads, as `product_doc.written` named its writer.
- **The fold**, `catervas_store::folder_docs::folder_docs(log)`: every proposal with its state (`pending`, `superseded`, `approved`, `returned`; the first owner's decision naming a proposal settles it, and a later proposal of the same path supersedes one not settled), every owner's decision in log order, and every folder change with the files it holds (a write's path; an approval's paths and twins) and whether it is integrated.
