## Goal

The owner of an existing repository starts Catervafication with one command, `catervafication_start`: Catervas files the epic "Catervafy the repository", approved by that order, opens a sprint that holds it alone when none is open, and, once the epic is under way, files one docs task per role that has a folder, an active agent and a reviewer, each from a fixed template, held to its own folder; the Marketing Specialist's waits until the Product Manager's product plan is accepted and integrated. A new project's lighter epic is filed by Catervas itself once step 06's product plan is approved, already approved by the owner's acceptance of that plan. Every role with a folder carries the kit skill `catervafying-the-repository`, the Developer also `catervas-recommended-workflow`, and the Scrum Master writes the cadence at that sprint's retro. Out of scope: every screen, the sprint review's refreshes and the line "Ada brought the architecture notes up to date with sprint 4" (07b); the DevOps Engineer's folder and skill (its phase).

## Decisions

- **Split.** Row 07 becomes 07 (this: the command, the query, the epic, its tasks, the kits) and 07b (the offer after setup, Today's and the Files page's card, the sprint review's refreshes and their line). Rejected: one plan of about 500 lines.
- **Fixed templates, filed by Catervas, not step 05's planning flow.** The work is the same on every project and needs no plan: an Architect's plan task and the Product Manager's contract sessions would cost three sessions to rewrite text Catervas already holds, and the Architect is itself one of the epic's writers. Rejected: Architect plan → PM contracts.
- **One module**, `crates/runtime/src/catervafication.rs` (`pub(crate)`): the templates (pure), the roles, `start`, the tasks' filing, the new project's rule. Rejected: the templates in `catervas-core`, which would need `default_reviewer_role` from `catervas-roles`, a crate core does not depend on.
- **The event** `catervafication.started { project: existing | new }`, recorded about the epic (its id on the envelope; `is_about_one_contract` true; no actor field: `task.created` names who filed it, `human` for the owner's Start, `catervas` for a new project). Schema `catervaficationStartedBody`, `additionalProperties: false`, `project` required. The projections pass over it (`projections.rs:682-772`). The web's types are generated from the schema (`packages/protocol-client/src/generate.ts`), and 07b gives the task page its words.
- **Filing the epic** is `catervas_store::requests::file_catervafication_epic`: `Filing` (`requests.rs:246`) gains `catervafies: Option<CatervaficationStartedBodyProject>`; `file` (`:254`) then inserts `kind: epic`, as it inserts `kind: task` for a parent, and `creation_bodies` (`:337`) appends, after `task.created`, `request.triaged { size: large, reason: "the Catervafication of an existing repository" | "the Catervafication of a new project", triaged_by: "catervas" }` and `catervafication.started { project }`, under the one `FILING` lock, so no triage session runs and the three events are one filing.

- **Start is the owner's approval.** `catervafication_start` (`command.schema.json`, `emptyBody`; `Command::CatervaficationStart`), handled by `human.rs` (`handle`, `:68`), under a `static STARTING: Mutex<()>` (`crate::locked`, `crates/runtime/src/lib.rs:111`). Refused, in order, nothing recorded: `catervafication_started: your team is already documenting this project, as <epic>`; `catervafication_new_project: a new project's Catervafication starts by itself once you approve its product plan` (the log holds `project.started`, step 06); `catervafication_no_product_manager: the team has no active Product Manager to move the epic into its plan; resume one first`; `catervafication_no_writers: no one on your team can both write and have their notes checked; add an Architect or a Scrum Master` when `catervafication_roles(team, Existing, ..)` has no Product Manager row (the Marketing Specialist is listed only with it, so the list is then empty). Then `start(.., Existing, "human", None)`, which files the epic, opens its sprint (below), asks `draft -> refining` as the Product Manager (`TransitionActor::ProductManager`, the first active one), as rule 10 asks it with no session (`requests.rs:117-127`), and, only when that moved, records `human.accepted { subject: contract, accepted_by: "human", message }`, the click's approval (`message` absent here). The acceptance comes after the move because `contract_accepted` (`crates/runtime/src/transitions.rs:1396`) counts only one after the last move into `refining`. The tick then judges it: `escalated` is refused (accepted) and `ready` is moved (`judge`, `requests.rs:238`). Start approves the epic alone; under `all` each task is approved as any contract. Said: `Your team starts documenting the project in <sprint>: <epic>.`, or without ` in <sprint>` when no sprint was opened. Rejected: an RPC method beside `team.start` (a human's order is a command, on `POST /command` and the browser's `command`, as `folder_doc_decide`); recording an approval at the epic's escalation, which would be an approval nobody gave then.
- **A sprint of its own.** `start` opens a sprint when none is open (`start_sprint(deps, None, by)`, `crates/runtime/src/sprints.rs:162`; `by` `human` for Start, `catervas` for a new project) and plans the epic into it as the governor (`plan_sprint(.., &PlannedBy::Governor)`, `:274`, which checks only that a sprint is open and the row in none), after catching the projections up. Its tasks then join it (`join_epics_sprint`, `:462`). **This is how nothing else is planned while the epic is open**: the sprint holds a task, so its planning ceremony never runs (`sprint_planning`, `rules.rs:483`, passes over a sprint that holds one), and the assignment gate takes only the open sprint's work (`in_the_open_sprint`, `crates/core/src/governor/gates.rs:189`: under the policy the Backlog waits; off it, only a task in the open sprint, or under an epic in none). Triage and refining of other requests go on, as preparation does (spec 3). With a sprint already open, nothing is opened or planned: the epic waits for a sprint as any epic does. Rejected: a readiness rule refusing other tasks (it spends a refine session and a readiness attempt per request, and three escalate); holding drafts in the tick (a second rule for what the sprint already does).
- **Which roles get a task**: `catervafication_roles(team, project, designer_ready) -> Vec<(Role, Role)>`, in this order, each with its reviewer: for `existing`, the Product Manager, the Architect, the Software Developer, the UI/UX Designer and the Marketing Specialist; for `new`, the Architect, the Software Developer and the Marketing Specialist (the product plan is step 06's task). For a new project the Product Manager has no row, so `catervafication_no_writers` does not apply; its list can still be empty (a Scrum Master and no Architect, one Developer, no Marketing Specialist), and the new project's rule then files nothing. The Scrum Master gets no task: it holds no writing tier and is never a task's assignee (01b); it writes `docs/catervas/delivery/cadence.md` with `catervas_write_folder_doc` at the retro (below). Two agents of a role share one task: a folder is the role's.
