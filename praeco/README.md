# Praeco

**A marketing agent that lives in your Slack and never stops working on your product's research, prospects and SEO, with every action it takes decided by code you can read.**

Praeco researches your product, the problem it solves, the people who have that problem and your competitors. It finds public posts and companies that show the need your product meets. It audits your site's search health and proposes fixes as pull requests. It works in bounded cycles, around the clock, within a budget that is a hard stop. It learns from your team's edits and from measured results, as rules you can read and undo. Nothing it does leaves your workspace without a person's approval, and it never contacts anyone on your behalf.

Open source under Apache 2.0, to run on your own server with your own Anthropic key, with a hosted service for teams that would rather not.
